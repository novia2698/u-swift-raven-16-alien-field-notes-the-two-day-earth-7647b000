## Why this line

The judge brief rewards, in order: a clear alien tourist's view of Earth, a laugh that comes from the alien's angle on ordinary life, an original detail few other entrants would reach, the shape of a real review (verdict, rating, closing remark), and tight writing with the punch at the end. This line does all five in one joke: polite-to-machines, afraid-of-neighbours, four stars, and a closing remark that only an alien tourist could make.
